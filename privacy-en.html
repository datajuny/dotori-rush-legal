2. Processing and Retention Periods

  • On-device progress, settings, recovery information and guest secret: kept on the device until the app is uninstalled or its data is cleared.
  • Kakao, Apple and Google login credentials, and authorization codes and access tokens from each service: used only to verify login or revoke the connection when an account is deleted, and not stored in the Operator’s database.
  • Single-use login verification value: usable only for five minutes after issuance.
  • App login token: valid for one hour after issuance.
  • Account, linked login identifiers, progress, game, ranking, reward and purchase records: retained until the user deletes the account in the app or the service ends.
  • News email address: retained until consent to receive emails is withdrawn or the account is deleted. On withdrawal, the email address is deleted and only the consent and withdrawal times remain. These records are also deleted when the account is deleted.
  • Where applicable law requires separate retention, the information is stored separately for the period required by that law.

Raw swipe inputs for a round are processed only during result validation and are not stored. Only the input count and SHA-256 hash are stored in the database.

5. Disposal and Account Deletion

Electronic files are deleted using methods that make recovery difficult. When you confirm deletion in Settings > Delete Account in the app, the server account and its linked login identifiers, sessions, progress, game, ranking, reward and purchase records are deleted together. The guest secret and login session on the device are also deleted, resetting on-device progress to its initial state. Deleted records and remaining hearts cannot be recovered. Store payment records remain with each store.

Consumable heart purchases are not granted again through purchase restoration. Deleting an account does not cancel automatic renewal of passes through the App Store or Google Play. You must cancel separately through pass management / cancellation in the app or the store’s subscription settings. Requests to delete information linked to RevenueCat can also be sent to the contact email, and the Operator will request deletion from that service.

6. Your Rights and How to Exercise Them

You may request access to, correction or deletion of your information, or suspension of its processing. You can delete your account directly in Settings > Delete Account in the app, or, if you have already uninstalled the app, send a request to datajuny01@gmail.com. After verifying your identity, we will handle your request without undue delay and inform you of the result. You can delete on-device information yourself by uninstalling the app or clearing its data in device settings. Turning off news emails in Settings > Receive News Emails in the app immediately withdraws your consent to receive them.

7. Automatic Storage Technologies

The app uses device storage (localStorage) for game progress, settings, the guest secret, login sessions and recovery of failed transmissions. The Operator has not separately installed advertising tracking pixels or its own analytics SDK. RevenueCat is used for purchase verification and purchase statistics. Google AdMob ad requests proceed after checking the privacy consent status. Where a privacy choices screen is required, it can be reopened through the advertising privacy settings in the app’s settings. On iOS, the system tracking permission status is checked before viewing ads, and non-personalized ads are requested if permission is not granted. Tracking permission is not required to use the game or receive ad rewards. Users can also reset advertising identifiers or limit personalized ads in device settings.

8. Security Measures

All server communications use HTTPS. The guest secret is stored on the server only as a SHA-256 hash. Kakao, Apple and Google login credentials are accepted only after their signatures, issuers, intended app, expiration times and single-use verification values are checked using each service’s public keys, and the credentials are not stored. Direct database writes are restricted using row-level security (RLS) and service roles. Round signatures, server-side replay and idempotent processing prevent forgery and duplicate settlement. Purchases and ad rewards are granted only after the server receives signed verification.
